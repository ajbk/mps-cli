# MPS Domain Context

## Purpose

This glossary defines the core domain language for the Memellow Programming System (MPS). MPS is a Teaching Intelligence System for designing Total Body Pilates classes. It is not an exercise library.

## Glossary

### MPS

Memellow Programming System. A Teaching Intelligence System that designs Pilates class plans from movement intent, strategy, exercise metadata, and safety constraints.

### Teaching Intelligence System

A system where the instructor teaches and the software designs the class structure. The system decides class strategy, exercise selection, sequencing, challenge, cues, and retest based on structured data and deterministic rules.

### Total Body Pilates Class

Every generated class trains the whole body. A class may emphasize one movement system, but it must still include supporting systems such as breath/core, spine, hip, legs, shoulder, and balance where appropriate.

### Movement System

A broad body-system category used to organize class intent and movement outcomes. MVP systems include Shoulder, Hip, Spine, Breath/Core, Legs, and Balance.

### Movement Experience

The clear movement improvement the student should feel after class. MVP experiences are Shoulder Freedom, Happy Hips, and Spine Reset.

### Movement Benchmark

A before/after assessment used to make movement improvement visible and felt. The same benchmark or equivalent retest is used at the end of class.

### Movement Strategy

The teaching plan derived from a Movement Experience and adjusted by observations. It defines primary focus, secondary focus, total-body emphasis distribution, key objectives, preferred exercise objectives, and explanation.

### Base Movement Strategy

The default strategy attached to a Movement Experience before observations are applied.

### Observation

Movement information entered by the instructor, such as thoracic stiffness or limited overhead reach. Observations modify strategy. They are not safety constraints.

### Observation Modifier

A data-driven rule stored in SQLite that maps observation keywords to strategy adjustments, added objectives, and preferred exercise objectives.

### Exercise

A Pilates movement unit with structured metadata. An exercise is selected because of its teaching role and movement effect, not merely because it belongs to an apparatus list.

### Exercise Role

The teaching purpose of an exercise in the class journey. Roles are Assess, Prepare, Prime, Integrate, Challenge, Transfer, and Restore.

### Prime Exercise

A main intervention exercise used in the BUILD phase to produce the target movement change.

### Movement Journey

The seven-phase class structure: ARRIVE, PREPARE, BUILD, INTEGRATE, CHALLENGE, TRANSFER, RESET & RETEST.

### ARRIVE

The phase that brings attention into the body and establishes the initial benchmark.

### PREPARE

The phase that prepares joints, breathing, motor control, and key muscles.

### BUILD

The main intervention phase. It must contain 2-4 Prime exercises.

### INTEGRATE

The phase that connects body systems so movement becomes coordinated rather than isolated.

### CHALLENGE

The phase that provides the approximate 20% challenge through balance, coordination, rotation, standing work, complexity, range, tempo, or load. Challenge does not simply mean harder exercises.

### TRANSFER

The phase that carries the movement quality into functional daily-life patterns.

### RESET & RETEST

The final phase that reduces tension and repeats the benchmark so students can feel improvement.

### 80% Success / 20% Challenge

The class should feel achievable most of the time while including an appropriate challenge dose. In the MVP this is enforced through level filtering, challenge-phase rules, regressions, and difficulty scoring.

### Equipment

Primary apparatus selected for the class. MVP primary apparatus are Reformer and Chair.

### Movement Context

A non-primary-apparatus context used for benchmark, transfer, reset, and retest. MVP contexts are Mat and Standing.

### Safety Constraint

A group-level contraindication or condition that affects exercise selection. Safety constraints are distinct from observations.

### Group Safety

MVP safety input that applies to the class as a whole. It includes contraindications and risk policy.

### Risk Policy

A safety behavior mode. MVP supports Conservative and Balanced. Conservative is the default.

### ClassRequest

The structured input submitted to the engine. It contains movement experience, level, duration, equipment, observations, and group safety constraints.

### ClassPlan

The canonical structured output generated by the Rust deterministic engine. JSON is the machine-readable representation. Markdown is the instructor-facing rendering.

### Deterministic Core

The Rust generation engine that creates the canonical ClassPlan using rules, scoring, SQLite data, and safety constraints. It must be testable and must not depend on an LLM.

### Optional LLM Enhancement

A future post-processing layer that may polish text but may not alter selected exercises, phase structure, safety rules, movement strategy, benchmarks, or duration allocation.
