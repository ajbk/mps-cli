# Monochrome gesture-ink Pilates style sheet

Preview: `data/reference/visual/style-sheets/mono-gesture-ink-pilates-v1.png`

This is a separate visual profile from the flat editorial style. It is intended for expressive movement cards, quote cards, and quiet Pilates illustrations.

## Locked decisions

- White paper background with generous negative space.
- Imperfect hand-drawn ink and pencil lines.
- Selective charcoal fill for clothing and shadow shapes.
- Minimal facial detail, while preserving glasses and the shoulder-length layered bob.
- Slightly elongated gesture is allowed for drawing rhythm; it must not redesign the teacher's body.
- Current outfit remains fixed: off-white thin-strap cropped Pilates camisole and dark charcoal high-waisted mid-thigh biker shorts.
- Apparatus is drawn with spare lines, but hand, foot, mat, carriage, bar, and strap contact must remain legible.

## Use this profile for

- One-pose quote or principle cards.
- Expressive balance and breath illustrations.
- Mat movement sketches.
- Reformer and Cadillac gesture studies.
- Editorial overview sheets with a quiet, personal tone.

## Do not copy

- The reference person's bun, face, clothing, or body proportions.
- Any quote or typography in the reference image unless supplied separately and cleared for use.
- The exact exercise pose when the flashcard's movement requirements differ.
